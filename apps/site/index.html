The direction

More ways to look after
what matters.

Planned capabilities.
These features are not available yet.

Compare before you clear

Review exact duplicates and similar photos, protect the ones you want and choose what goes. Similar does not always mean spare.

Keep another copy

Back up selected photos or albums, with automatic backup as a separate choice. A copy must be verified before it is called backed up.

Make something from it

Reference-guided photo edits, text photos into documents and video edits shaped by your prompt. Keep the originals alongside new versions.

Find a moment again

Organise screenshots and return to meaningful anniversary memories, with room to keep the everyday clutter out of the way.

The design rules

Clear choices.
Careful boundaries.

Phone-library access, cloud AI and backup serve different purposes. The design keeps their permissions separate, so viewing your photos does not require turning on a cloud feature.

An app lock is planned. It will protect opening Still; it will not hide ordinary phone-library photos from other apps. Cloud providers, retention details and release privacy information still need to be settled before those features launch.

This website preview has no signup, analytics or media uploads. These design rules describe the intended app; they are not a released app privacy policy.
