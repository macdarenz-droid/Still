# Android listing draft

Status: unpublished working copy for the foundation milestone, 19 October 2026.
This is not a claim that a store-ready build or listing exists. Still is a working
name pending app-store, domain and trademark checks in the intended markets.

## Foundation copy

Use the following only after the named behaviour passes release acceptance on the
actual Android candidate. Remove any feature that has not passed those checks.
Future AI, backup, cleanup, memories and app-lock features are not advertised here.

**App name:** Still

**Short description (59 characters):**

Browse, find and share the photos and videos on your phone.

**Full description:**

Still is an everyday gallery for the photos and videos on your Android phone.

Browse your library, explore albums, search available media and open photos or
videos in a full-screen viewer. Select items to share through Android and keep
favourites within Still.

Choose the photo and video access available on your Android version. Still shows
the media it has permission to access. If you change that access, the available
library changes too.

Favourites are stored within Still; they do not change your phone's system
favourites. Viewing media does not require an account. The foundation build does
not connect to a backup provider or send media to an AI service.

Still is being developed as a native Android gallery. Supported devices and
release availability will be confirmed before publication.

## Claim boundaries

- Keep this copy tied to the exact accepted build. A successful compile does not
  establish browsing performance, accessibility, permission handling or store
  eligibility.
- Do not call Still a universal default-gallery replacement. Android system/OEM
  gallery privileges and open-with behaviour differ across devices.
- Do not describe app favourites as system favourites or imply cloud syncing.
- Do not use "private vault", "encrypted gallery", "all photos stay on your
  device", "zero data collected", "unlimited backup" or provider guarantees
  without a verified implementation and the appropriate disclosures.
- A future app lock protects opening Still. It does not hide ordinary
  phone-library media from other apps and does not establish vault encryption.
- Add later capabilities only when implemented, independently reviewed, tested
  on the final candidate and disclosed. Backup and AI processing remain distinct.

## Store assets still required

Real accepted-build screenshots on representative devices, final small-icon and
monochrome artwork, accurate feature graphic, support contact and published app
privacy policy. Illustration studies from the website must not be submitted as
app screenshots. Store metadata, content rating, Data safety disclosures and any
photo/video permission declaration must match the final binary and dependencies.

The owner controls store submission and publication. No store listing, signing or
publication is part of this foundation task.
